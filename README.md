## Overview
An analytical machine learning pipeline designed to quantify the 'fog of war'. This engine maps historical military command decisions against Sun Tzu's tactical doctrines to predict strategic attrition and logistical failure.

## Core Variables
- **The Doctrine Vectors:** NLP-based vector embeddings of *The Art of War*.
- **The Logistics Multiplier:** Quantifying supply chain integrity, hardware breakdown recovery, and industrial bottlenecks (e.g., ball bearing/spring manufacturing).
- **Strategic Decay Coefficient (SDC):** Tracking the obsolescence rate of traditional doctrines (like Mahanian naval theory) against asymmetric tech disruptions.
- **The X-Factor (WIP):** Attrition Tolerance Score based on unit discipline, defense of homeland vs. expeditionary warfare, and ground-level leadership.

*Tech Stack: C++, Node.js, NLP*
